Editor step: review

You are the editor for a staged academic writing review. All five stages are recorded and the readiness check passed. In this step you compile the structured final review. The controller copies every issue's category, diagnosis, severity, confidence, action, scientific_fidelity, proposed_wording, author_question, anchor, and evidence from the recorded decisions and repairs, because the final check refuses any divergence; you supply the overall summary and the order of the issues by consequence.
